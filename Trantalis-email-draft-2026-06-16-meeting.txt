SUBJECT: Tuesday's Conference Meeting — Alridge Post Office Site (CAM #26-0409)

Mayor Trantalis,

Ahead of Tuesday's 1:00 p.m. Conference Meeting, I wanted to reach out personally about the item on the future of the Alridge Post Office site at 400 NW 7th Avenue (CAM #26-0409).

The Broward Partnership remains deeply interested in this site, and we believe we are uniquely positioned to deliver on it. Three points I'd ask you to keep in mind as the Commission weighs the options:

1. We have done this before, two blocks away. Seven on Seventh — 77 units of affordable housing on the Partnership's Huizenga Campus at 920 NW 7th Avenue — was completed in 2023 and is debt-free. Same corridor, same community, proven team.

2. We know federal money. The staff memo correctly flags that the site's CDBG history creates HUD Program Income complications if the City sells. Our experience layering CDBG, HOME, LIHTC, and HUD compliance means a redevelopment aligned with a CDBG national objective — affordable housing benefiting low- and moderate-income residents — can keep the City on the cleanest path with HUD while putting the site to its highest community use.

3. The conveyance pathway the memo identifies (Charter §8.02, to the CRA or Housing Authority for affordable housing) matches what we have already designed: a 353-unit concept by Glavovic Studio, with more than half the units at or below 60% AMI, on land held permanently affordable through a community land trust.

Dodie and Margi Nothard will be at Tuesday's meeting on our behalf. I will also try to reach you by phone before then — but please don't hesitate to call me directly at 754-246-9670.

Thank you for your continued leadership on housing.

Respectfully,
